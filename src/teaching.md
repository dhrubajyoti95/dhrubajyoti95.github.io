# Teaching

## Vision
  <p align="justify">
  For me, teaching extends beyond explaining concepts or solving problems; it is about mentoring students to develop intellectual independence, analytical reasoning, and confidence in tackling unfamiliar challenges. Engaging with students often offers fresh perspectives on well-established ideas, prompting deeper reflection and occasionally revealing new ways of thinking about familiar problems. In this sense, teaching and research are mutually enriching pursuits, each strengthening the other.</p>
